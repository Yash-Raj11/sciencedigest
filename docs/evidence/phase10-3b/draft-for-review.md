- Food goes into one column.  `[-]`
- The energy people burn, at rest and on the move, goes into the other.

## Protocol layer

**Paragraph 1**

- For 2 weeks, everyone kept their usual sleep at home.
  - `baseline_and_intervention_periods`: “After a 2-week habitual sleep period at baseline, participants were randomized to either 2-week sleep extension (sleep extension group) or 2-week continued habitual sleep (control group) (Figure 1).”
- Then chance split them into 2 groups for 2 more weeks.
  - `randomization_method`: “Block randomization, stratified by sex, was performed using computer-generated random numbers.”
  - `baseline_and_intervention_periods`: “After a 2-week habitual sleep period at baseline, participants were randomized to either 2-week sleep extension (sleep extension group) or 2-week continued habitual sleep (control group) (Figure 1).”
- The sleep group had a personal sleep advice session, aiming for 8.5 hours in bed each night.
  - `sleep_hygiene_counseling_delivery`: “Those who were randomized to the sleep extension group received individualized sleep hygiene counseling through a structured interview (E.T.) (eMethods in Supplement 2).”
  - `sleep_extension_target`: “At the end of the interview, participants were provided with individualized recommendations to follow at home for 2 weeks, with the aim of extending their bedtime duration to 8.5 hours.”

**Paragraph 2**

- Nobody got a diet or an exercise plan.
  - `free_living_routine`: “Participants continued their daily routine activities at home without any prescribed diet or physical activity.”
- A wrist sensor recorded sleep for all 4 weeks.
  - `sleep_monitoring_duration`: “Sleep-wake patterns were continuously monitored at home by wrist actigraphy throughout the 4-week study.”
  - `actigraphy_device`: “Participants were asked to wear an accelerometer (motion)-based monitor (Actiwatch Spectrum Plus; Philips) and to press a built-in event marker button when they went to bed to sleep each night and when they got out of be…”
- A home scale they could not read weighed them twice every morning.
  - `hidden_scale_weights`: “Participants were provided a cellular-enabled weight scale (BodyTrace; BodyTrace Inc) and instructed to take their nude weights twice every morning after awakening before eating or drinking. Weight values were hidden fro…”
- Researchers measured the energy participants burned, and worked out food eaten from that plus changes in body stores.
  - `dlw_method`: “Total energy expenditure was measured by the doubly labeled water method.”
  - `energy_intake_method`: “For each 2-week period, the energy intake was calculated from the sum of total energy expenditure and change in body energy stores using the principle of energy balance.”
  - `body_energy_stores_method`: “For each 2-week period, the change in body energy stores was computed from the regression (slope, grams per day) of daily home weights and change in body composition (ie, fat mass and fat-free mass) using dual-energy x-r…”

**Limitation 1**

- The control group’s rise in intake and weight may have added to the gap.
  - `limit_control_increase`: “The increase in energy intake and weight from baseline that we observed in the control group may have contributed to the significant treatment effects.”

**Limitation 2**

- The change lasted 2 weeks, so nobody knows how long people keep the longer nights.
  - `baseline_and_intervention_periods`: “After a 2-week habitual sleep period at baseline, participants were randomized to either 2-week sleep extension (sleep extension group) or 2-week continued habitual sleep (control group) (Figure 1).”
  - `limit_short_duration_sustainability`: “The study did not provide information on how long healthy sleep habits could be maintained over longer periods.”

**Limitation 3**

- The method that measured the energy participants burned is precise to about 5%, which adds uncertainty to each intake figure.
  - `limit_dlw_precision`: “The doubly labeled water method has a precision of 5%, which may translate into some degree of uncertainty in the energy intake calculations.”

**Limitation 4**

- Only adults with overweight who met strict rules took part, so the result may not hold for others.
  - `limit_generalizability`: “We enrolled adults with overweight and used selective eligibility criteria, which may limit generalizability to more diverse populations.”

## Takeaways

**Takeaway 1**

- Aiming for 8.5 hours in bed, adults who had slept under 6.5 hours got 1.2 hours more sleep than the control group.
  - `eligibility_age_bmi_sleep`: “Adult men and women aged 21 to 40 years with a body mass index (calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared) between 25.0 and 29.9 and a mean habitual sleep duration of less than 6.5 hours per n…”
  - `sleep_extension_target`: “At the end of the interview, participants were provided with individualized recommendations to follow at home for 2 weeks, with the aim of extending their bedtime duration to 8.5 hours.”
  - `sleep_duration_diff`: “Participants in the sleep extension group had a significant increase from baseline in mean sleep duration by actigraphy compared with those in the control group (1.2 hours; 95% CI, 1.0-1.4 hours; P < .001).”

**Takeaway 2**

- Compared with the control group, they ate 270.4 kcal a day less, with no diet.

  - `free_living_routine`: “Participants continued their daily routine activities at home without any prescribed diet or physical activity.”

**Takeaway 3**

- The authors noted that limiting electronic devices seemed to matter to participants; the trial did not test it on its own.
  - `limit_sleep_factors_unassessed`: “We did not systematically assess the factors that may have influenced sleep behavior, but limiting the use of electronic devices appeared to be a key intervention among the participants (eTable 4 in Supplement 2).”
